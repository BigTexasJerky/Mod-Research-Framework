# CATEGORIES

Use these when they make sense:

- `automation`
- `endgame`
- `extraction`
- `logistics`
- `materials`
- `misc`
- `power`
- `processing`
- `storage`
- `thermal`
- `utility`

If your mod does not make sense under a vanilla-style progression branch, use `misc`.

Do not force a ridiculous prerequisite just because you want a card somewhere. A clean no-prerequisite MISC entry is better than telling somebody their shovel mod somehow requires quantum physics.

MRF can discover a custom category ID, but public mods should stick to the shared list unless there is a good reason not to. The whole point is organization.
